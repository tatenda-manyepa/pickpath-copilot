# RB-05 Conveyor jam

**Applies to:** CNV-501, CNV-510, CNV-520
**Typical symptom:** a pick station cannot send totes away; outbound spur full.

## Steps
1. Find the jam point from the first CNV-501 or CNV-510 alarm.
2. Stop the conveyor section at its local panel and lock it out.
3. Clear the jammed totes. Check the photo-eye at the merge for alignment and a loose bracket.
4. Restart the section and watch three totes pass the merge.
5. If this merge has jammed more than twice in 90 days, raise or escalate a maintenance request. **Merge M2** is a known repeat offender (MR-2207).
